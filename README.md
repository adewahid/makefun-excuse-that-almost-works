## The entry

One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week — a
dentist appointment booked months ago, with a cancellation fee. Boring,
checkable, money on the line: the kind of excuse a tired manager has
heard before and almost believes.

> Sorry, dentist appointment this morning, booked it months ago and they charge if I cancel, in after lunch

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (105 characters,
18 words — inside the 1–140 character gate and the 20-word cap).

